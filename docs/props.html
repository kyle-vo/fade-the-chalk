Scorecard every graded call, 1u at the price + fee

The prop rule. Main line = the strike priced nearest 50¢. BET = a player on the underdog (his team under 45% to win on Kalshi), with one of: RB rushing yards UNDER, QB rushing yards UNDER, TE receiving yards UNDER, QB passing yards OVER. That made +11.8% on 358 bets at the price at kickoff plus the fee, and it was positive in all 6 weeks (+3, +17, +21, +12, +4, +13%). It was found on weeks 1-4 and held on weeks 5-6, which weren't used to find it. The same four props on favorites were break-even, so they show as PASS. Edges are single digits per bet: it pays over many bets, not on one. Not a bet: first-TD and 2+ TD props (they lost 35-47% every week), overs in general (−9%), and our own projections vs the line (they lost at every threshold). For anytime TDs, use the BET / AVOID calls on the main board.
💰 Crowd money = the crowd's taker dollars on the main line before kickoff. The signal: rushing yards with 60%+ of the money on the over ($2k+ traded) → the UNDER, and passing yards with 60%+ on the over and $20k+ traded → the OVER. The crowd mostly bets overs: on rushing yards it loses, on the busiest passing lines it wins. STRONG BET = the underdog rule and the money signal agree: 120-70, +18.6% (weeks 3-6 +16.1%, weeks 7-11 +21.6%). BET without the money signal: 247-213, +1.2% (−5.9% in weeks 7-11), so size those smaller. LEAN = the passing-yards signal on a team that isn't the underdog (19-11, +15%, a small sample). The rushing signal on its own was break-even (154-140), so it stays PASS. Fading the money on receiving yards, receptions and TDs flipped between halves of the season or lost. Crowd $ on over for this week's games is the money so far (pulled for games in the next 36 hours, growing until kickoff), so a STRONG BET can appear or drop: recheck close to the game. Finished weeks use all the money before kickoff.
