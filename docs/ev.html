How it works. Pinnacle is the sharpest book, so its price with the vig removed is the best estimate of each side's real chance (Pinnacle fair). EV = that chance times what you'd be paid, minus your stake: +3% means you expect to make 3¢ per $1 over the long run. For Kalshi / Robinhood, the price is the ask plus Kalshi's taker fee (about 1.75¢ at 50¢). Robinhood charges a flat 2¢ per contract instead, so knock about a point off there. Sportsbook prices are their posted moneylines. Stake = a quarter of the Kelly bet for your bankroll, the standard way to size +EV without big swings. Pick the books you actually use; the choice is saved on this device. Read it with care. Prices refresh with each run (hourly, or press ↻ Rerun), Pinnacle at most every 50 minutes, so a big edge can be a stale line that's already moved. Check the live price before you bet. "Alt line" = Kalshi's strike differs from Pinnacle's, so the fair is adjusted and less exact. Kalshi asks are only good for the size posted at that price.

CLV (closing-line value) = what the bet was worth at Pinnacle's last price before the game. Beating the close over and over is the proof an edge is real: it shows up after a couple hundred bets, long before wins and losses settle it. Units = 1u flat on every flagged side at the price it was flagged.
