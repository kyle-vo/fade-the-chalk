New board, nothing proven yet. Each row is one side of a market. Robinhood ask = what a $1 contract on that side costs (Robinhood's NBA contracts are Kalshi's), with the American odds. Pinnacle fair = the sharpest book's de-vigged chance at the same line. Edge = fair minus ask, in points. Crowd share = the share of the crowd's taker dollars on that side (each pre-game trade credited to the side the bettor actually backed), frozen at tip-off. LEAN · untested on a spread = the side with less of the crowd's money once $25k+ is in: the rule that has worked on NFL spreads (29 of 42 covered), tried here on basketball. VALUE · untested on a moneyline = Robinhood selling a team 3+ points under Pinnacle's fair price. Neither has an NBA record yet: the scorecard below grades them from the first game, next to every-favorite and every-underdog baselines, and real rules get set once there are a few weeks of games. Small stakes until then.
