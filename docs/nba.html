Both sides of every spread. Robinhood ask = what a $1 contract on that side costs. Pinnacle fair = the sharpest book's de-vigged chance at the same line; Edge = fair minus ask, in points. Crowd share / $ on side = how much of the crowd's taker money (each pre-game trade credited to the side the bettor backed) is on that side, frozen at tip-off. Crowd money alone was break-even on NBA spreads last season, so it's information, not a pick.
One row per game. Moneyline = what a $1 Robinhood contract on each team costs (Robinhood's NBA contracts are Kalshi's), with the American odds, and Pinnacle's de-vigged fair chance under it. Spread = the favorite's line and price, the underdog's under it. Crowd $ = how the crowd's taker dollars split on the moneyline (left bar = away team), frozen at tip-off. Rules, tested on all of last season (1,086 regular-season games, Kalshi's price at tip plus the fee): BET on a spread = the team that won its last game by 20+. It covered 58% (+14%), and held in both halves of the season (+10% Oct-Jan, +18% Feb-Apr) and across favorites, underdogs, home and road. The market prices in a letdown after a blowout that doesn't come. STRONG BET = the same, when its opponent also won its last game (+23%, about 125 games). LEAN on a moneyline = the same team (+8%; the spread pays better). LEAN on a moneyline or spread also = the better team priced as the underdog: its season point differential is 4+ points a game better than its opponent's (5+ games each), yet it's the underdog. Last season: +39% on the moneyline, +22% on the spread, both halves of the season, and stronger as the gap grew, but only about 57 games all season, so it's a LEAN. LEAN on a total = the under when the total is below 220 (+18%, only 74 games). What did not work last season: fading the crowd's spread money (the NFL rule, +0.6% on 1,128 games), the crowd's moneyline money, back-to-backs, win streaks, home/road. The rule needs each team's previous game, so it starts with each team's second game. Regular season only: in the playoffs it was flat.
