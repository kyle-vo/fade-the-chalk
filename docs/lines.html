No model here. Each row is one side of a spread or total. Robinhood ask = what a $1 contract on that side costs. Pinnacle fair = the sharpest book's de-vigged chance at the same line (a ~ means Pinnacle's line was a whole number, Robinhood only lists half points, so the nearest strike is shown and the fair price is nudged for the half point). Edge = fair minus ask, in points; BET at 2+, STRONG BET at 5+: you are simply being paid more than the sharpest book says the side is worth. Crowd $ on this side = share of the taker dollars on this exact market from the trade tape (each trade credited to the side the bettor backed); Tape $ = both sides together; $ traded = the whole ladder of lines for this game. Rows lock at first pitch / kickoff (volumes only ever go up before that) and grade from the final score. The scorecard at the bottom is the point: does the side with more of the crowd's money cover less than its price says?
